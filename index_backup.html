About the Project

What Is EcoScan VIT?

A real-time campus sustainability tool that turns every barcode scan into actionable carbon data — building a collective picture of VIT's environmental footprint.

Real-Time Number Scanner
OpenCV + EasyOCR + PyTesseract read the printed 13-digit code below barcodes in real time on your camera feed.
Indian Barcode Aware
Scanner is tuned for Indian EAN-13 patterns (leftmost digit typically 8, country prefix 890) for higher lock accuracy.
Lifecycle CO2 Science
Carbon data from IPCC, ecoinvent & Quantis LCA studies. 60+ product categories with 5-stage pipeline breakdown.
VIT Student Login
Sign in with your @vitstudent.ac.in Google account. Your scans are saved and contribute to campus-wide analytics.
Live Campus Dashboard
Real-time leaderboard, department breakdown, product category donut chart, and 7-day scan trend — all from Firestore.
Green Alternatives
Every scan suggests 3 lower-carbon alternatives with % savings — nudging students toward sustainable choices.
Process

How It Works

1
Scan the Number Row
Open EcoScan on your phone browser and align the printed 13-digit label below the barcode. Python OCR locks automatically.
2
Carbon Calculation
We look up the product in Open Food Facts + UPC Item DB, then apply IPCC lifecycle data to compute CO2e for all 5 stages.
3
Saved to Campus DB
Your scan is saved to Firebase Firestore under your VIT student ID, contributing to the campus sustainability map.
4
Campus Dashboard
Aggregated data shows VIT's total CO2 footprint by department, category, and day — driving collective action.
