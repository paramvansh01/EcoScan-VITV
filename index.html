About the Project

What Is EcoScan VIT?

A real-time campus sustainability tool that turns every barcode scan into actionable carbon data.

Real-Time Scanner

OpenCV + EasyOCR + PyTesseract read printed 13-digit codes below barcodes in real time on your camera feed.

Indian Barcode Aware

Tuned for Indian EAN-13 patterns (prefix 890) for higher lock accuracy on local products.

Lifecycle CO₂ Science

Carbon data from IPCC, ecoinvent & Quantis LCA studies. 60+ product categories with 5-stage pipeline.

VIT Student Login

Sign in with @vitstudent.ac.in. Your scans contribute to campus-wide analytics.

Live Campus Dashboard

Real-time leaderboard, department breakdown, and 7-day scan trend — all from Firestore.

Green Alternatives

Every scan suggests 3 lower-carbon alternatives with % savings — nudging toward sustainable choices.
